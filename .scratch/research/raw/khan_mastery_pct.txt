How is my mastery percentage calculated?
Updated
August 25, 2026 21:27
Follow
Your
mastery percentage
shows the percentage of skills in a course or unit that you've reached
Proficient or Mastered
on.
Both Proficient and Mastered skills count toward your mastery percentage.
Skills at any other level—Not started, Attempted, or Familiar—do not count toward the percentage.
This calculation applies to learners using both Classic Khan Academy and Khan Academy Reimagined.
Using Khan Academy Reimagined?
You may see a label that says "X of Y skills to Proficient." Skills at both the Proficient
and
Mastered levels count toward this number.
How is my mastery percentage calculated?
For example, if a course has 20 skills and you've reached Proficient or Mastered on 5 of them, your mastery percentage is
25%
:
5 Proficient or Mastered skills ÷ 20 total skills = 25% mastery
How do I increase my mastery percentage?
To increase your mastery percentage, level up more skills to Proficient or Mastered by completing activities such as:
Practice exercises
Quizzes
Unit tests
Course Challenges
After completing an activity, you may see which skills leveled up, leveled down, or stayed at the same level.
Keep in mind that skill levels can move up or down based on your performance as you continue practicing, so your mastery percentage can change too.
